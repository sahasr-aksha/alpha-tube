# ⚠️ Disclaimer

## About Alpha Tube

Alpha Tube is a **free, open-source video downloader** for Windows that uses [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) and [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org/) to download and process videos from various online platforms.
